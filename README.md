# IPTV Subscription Provider: Clear Pricing, Easy Trial

Choosing an **IPTV subscription provider** can look deceptively simple. You see a long channel list, a monthly price, maybe a free trial, and suddenly every website starts looking like it was designed by the same committee.

The useful comparison is a little more boring—and much more practical. Look at the pricing structure, connection limits, supported devices, trial terms, refund policy, and, most importantly, whether the provider clearly identifies the rights it has to distribute the content it advertises.

One service that currently draws attention for its unusually large published catalogue is **TiviLive**. Its website advertises more than 28,000 live channels and more than 100,000 video-on-demand titles, along with EPG listings, sports and PPV programming, and playback up to 4K/UHD/FHD depending on the service and device.

Yes. TiviLive currently advertises a **24-hour free trial** and says that no credit card is required. The provider says the trial is intended to let users test the service before purchasing a paid subscription.

The pricing page also advertises a **7-day money-back guarantee** on paid plans.

Those two terms are useful when comparing an IPTV subscription provider because video performance can vary with device hardware, network quality and geographic location. A service that works smoothly on one setup may behave very differently on another.

## What Devices Does It Support?

TiviLive says it supports a broad range of platforms, including Android devices, iPhone, Smart TVs, Firestick, MAG Box, Windows, macOS and popular IPTV applications.

The site also promotes Kodi support and describes the service as usable across TV, phone and computer screens.

For buyers, the important detail is not simply the number of supported devices. It is whether the exact player, operating system and network combination you use performs reliably.

## What Content Does the Channel List Show?

This is where the comparison gets more complicated.

TiviLive's published channel directory contains large numbers of sports, entertainment, news and regional categories. It also lists entries associated with recognizable services and programming brands, including Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, ESPN+, Peacock, Paramount+, Sky, DAZN and others.

That is a significant verification point for anyone researching an **IPTV subscription provider**.

The site's public pages reviewed here do not provide enough licensing documentation to independently establish that the provider is authorized to redistribute every branded channel or programme listed in its catalogue. A large channel count therefore should not automatically be treated as proof that every stream is properly licensed.

In the United States, the Copyright Office has stated that unauthorized streaming can implicate copyright owners' public-performance rights, and federal authorities have prosecuted commercial IPTV schemes involving unauthorized retransmission of copyrighted television programming.

That does not mean IPTV technology itself is unlawful. The key distinction is whether a provider has the necessary rights to distribute the specific content it offers.

## How to Compare an IPTV Subscription Provider

A useful checklist is surprisingly short.

### 1. Check the real total price

Do not stop at the monthly headline price. Look at the full subscription term, number of simultaneous connections, taxes or fees where applicable, and whether the price changes at renewal.

### 2. Check the trial and refund terms

TiviLive currently advertises a 24-hour trial and a seven-day money-back policy. Read the actual conditions before relying on either one.

### 3. Check what “connection” means

A one-connection subscription means one simultaneous stream. TiviLive explicitly defines additional connections as the option for households that want more than one screen running at the same time.

### 4. Check the content rights

This is the part that giant channel numbers can easily distract from. A long list is not the same thing as documented distribution rights.

### 5. Test your own setup

Internet speed, Wi-Fi stability, device age and playback software can all affect the viewing experience. A trial is more useful when you test the channels and devices you actually plan to use rather than simply opening the service for five minutes.

## Are There Any Verified Promo Codes?

A search of the provider's current public pages did not surface a clearly documented public coupon code. The offers that could be directly verified were the **24-hour free trial** and the **7-day money-back guarantee**.

That is worth keeping in mind when you see third-party pages advertising large “exclusive” discounts or coupon codes. The safest comparison is always between a claimed offer and the price actually shown during checkout.

## Final Thoughts on Choosing an IPTV Subscription Provider

TiviLive currently publishes a broad subscription menu, with prices ranging from **$15 for one month and one connection to $140 for 12 months and five connections**. Its website also advertises a large live-TV and VOD catalogue, multi-device support, a 24-hour trial and a seven-day refund window.

But price and channel count are only part of the decision.

When comparing any IPTV subscription provider, the more useful questions are straightforward: Does the service have clearly documented rights to the content? Does the trial work on your actual device? Does the connection plan match how many screens you use? And are the refund and payment terms clear?

The giant channel number gets the headline. The boring details usually determine whether the subscription makes sense.
